HOW TO PLAY

TAP A TILE TO ROTATE IT CLOCKWISE

CONNECT

Every arm must meet a facing arm on the neighbouring tile, and nothing may point off the board.

SEPARATE

No arm may meet a facing arm, and nothing may point off the board. Arms must end against a blank side.

PROGRESSION

  • Five stages per group; the grid grows every few groups.
  • Stage 5 is the group peak.
  • NEXT unlocks only once the current level is solved.

HINTS

  • A hint snaps one tile to its solved rotation.
  • You get 3 hints on first entry into a new group.
  • Replaying a cleared group does not refill them.
  • Out of hints? The same button becomes "+ EARN HINT" and a watched ad grants +1.
